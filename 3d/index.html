Model notes
  • Force: a traveling-wave push along the floor under the left half of the bath, decaying with height as exp(−2d/δ). Skin depth δ = √(1 / (π f μ₀ σ)) with σ = 3.8 MS/m for liquid aluminum (approximate). The cyan box over the stirrer is drawn δ tall.
  • Flow: 3D incompressible solver (stable fluids) on a 56 × 22 × 28 grid with 36 mm cells. Walls and floor are no-slip and the top is free-slip. Vorticity confinement and weak drag stand in for turbulence.
  • At high frequency δ is smaller than one cell. The solver keeps the total thrust but cannot resolve the thin layer, so treat high-frequency results as rough.
  • Cross flow is the fastest motion across the bath width. It comes from the stirrer being narrower than the bath and from friction on the side walls. A 2D model has none.
  • Wall force density is held constant. Frequency changes penetration depth only. Stirrer width, bath length and the 0.20 m pole pitch are assumptions, not machine data.
  • Left out: free-surface motion, thermal convection, the refractory gap, and any calibration against real installations. Qualitative only. Do not use it to size or predict a real installation.
