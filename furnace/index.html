Model notes
  • Machine presets: "Low-frequency bottom stirrer" is a generic, illustrative class of bottom-mounted linear induction stirrer driven at roughly 0.1–5 Hz through an air gap of refractory and plate. The values are rounded and are not product specifications. "Compact demo" is the original 2 m toy.
  • Force: a traveling-wave push in the melt above the stirrer, decaying with height as exp(−2·a·d). The gap adds a factor exp(−2·a·g) before the melt. The decay rate a comes from the skin depth δ = √(1 / (π f μ₀ σ)) with σ = 3.8 MS/m (approximate), softened by slip (a moving melt sees a lower slip frequency: δ/√slip). The optional Field model adds the pole-pitch limit: α² = (π/τ)² + 2i·slip/δ², a = Re α. Current scales force as I².
  • Thrust is normalised so that the preset gap, frequency and pole pitch give the nominal force; the readout shows the force reaching the melt as a percentage of that design value.
  • Flow: 2D incompressible solver (stable fluids, MacCormack dye advection) with no-slip walls and floor and a free-slip top. Vorticity confinement and weak drag stand in for turbulence. The model is a vertical slice through the stirrer centreline, so it has no cross flow and mixes faster than the 3D page.
  • Field travel speed is 2·τ·f from the Pole pitch slider. Sim time is compressed so that 5–10 minute reversal cycles can be watched.
  • Left out: 3D effects, free-surface motion, thermal convection, plate heating, side-mounted installations and any calibration against a real furnace. Qualitative only. Do not use it to size or predict a real installation.
